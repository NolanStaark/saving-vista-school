Have a Letter to Share?

If you've received a letter or notice from the school that you think other families or the community should see, you can submit it below — anonymously if you prefer. Every submission is reviewed by our team privately first; nothing is posted to this page automatically.

Submit a Letter

To protect your privacy if you choose to stay anonymous:
  • Don't write your name inside the document itself.
  • If submitting a photo of a letter, be aware phone photos can carry hidden location data — consider taking a screenshot of the photo instead, or removing location data before uploading.
  • We do not ask for your name or email on this form.

Note: like virtually any online form, the hosting service may retain basic technical logs (such as an IP address) that we do not see or use — if you need stronger protection, message us directly to discuss more secure options.
