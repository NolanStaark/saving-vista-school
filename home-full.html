Standing Up for Vista School Families

Saving Vista School is an independent, parent-led effort to bring transparency and accountability to the Vista School Board and Vista's current administration — through documented letters of concern, open access to meetings, and shared resources for families.

Read the Letters See the Meeting Schedule

Why This Site Exists

This site was organized by a group of concerned parents who believe a number of recent decisions by the Vista School Board and Vista's current administration deserve closer scrutiny. We're engaging directly with the Vista School Board and the Utah State Charter School Board to push for transparency and accountability — and this is where we're keeping that effort public: letters sent, meetings held, and the policies that are supposed to protect families.
