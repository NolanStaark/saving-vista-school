About Vista School

Vista School is a free, public charter school in Ivins, Utah, serving students in grades K–9 with an emphasis on Arts, Technology, and STEM academics (source: vistautah.com). This background is provided for context only — Saving Vista School is not affiliated with or speaking on behalf of Vista School.

Our Mission

Saving Vista School is a nonprofit organized by a group of concerned parents. We believe a number of recent decisions by the school's administration deserve closer scrutiny, and we are engaging directly with Vista's Charter Board and the Utah State Charter School Board to push for transparency and accountability.
